    About this map

    Covers about 2,200 km² of the Central Plateau around Lake Péligre, Mirebalais and Hinche. Each 20 m pixel is classed as Farmland, Forest, Water or Open land for the 2025 growing season. A random-forest model reads Sentinel-2 imagery from three periods: before planting (Feb–Mar), mid-season (May–Jun) and after harvest (Aug–Sep).

    How far to trust it. The model learned from labelled fields inside this same area. In cross-validation it gets about 3 in 4 pixels right, so read small areas with care. Confidence is the model's own probability for the class shown; faded colours on the map mark pixels under 50%. An independent accuracy check against ground-truth points is in progress.

    Seasons vary. The image periods follow the Central Plateau calendar: first season about March–June, a dry July, second season about August–November. Fields planted off this calendar, or left unplanted in a dry year, can be misread as Open land.

    Classes. “Open land” (the model's Bare class) means not farmed and not tree-covered: grassland, shrubland, bare soil, rock, roads and buildings. “Potential farmland” is Farmland plus Open land, the land that could be farmed.

    Your data. Areas you draw or upload stay in your browser and are never uploaded. No farmer or plot data is in this site.
