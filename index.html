    About this map

    Each 20 m pixel is classed as Farmland, Forest, Water or Bare for the 2025 growing season. A random-forest model reads Sentinel-2 imagery from three periods: before planting (Feb–Mar), mid-season (May–Jun) and after harvest (Aug–Sep).

    Where to trust it. The model learned from labelled fields in one part of the Central Plateau (dashed outline). Farther away it meets landscapes it has never seen: towns, mangroves, humid forest in the north, dry scrub in the northwest. Read results there as rough. Accuracy against independent ground-truth points has not been published yet.

    Seasons vary. The three image periods follow the Central Plateau calendar: first season about March–June, a dry July, second season about August–November. The north gets earlier winter rain and grows longer-maturing crops, and rainfall shifts from year to year. Fields planted off this calendar can be misread, often as Bare.

    Classes. “Bare” means not farmed and not tree-covered. It includes grassland, shrubland, rock, roads and buildings, because the model has no separate built-up class. “Potential farmland” is Farmland + Bare, the land that could be farmed.

    Your data. Areas you draw or upload stay in your browser and are never uploaded. No farmer or plot data is in this site.
