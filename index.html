    About this map

    Covers about 2,200 km² of the Central Plateau around Lake Péligre, Mirebalais and Hinche. Each 20 m pixel is classed as Farmland, Forest, Water or Bare for the 2025 growing season. A random-forest model reads Sentinel-2 imagery from three periods: before planting (Feb–Mar), mid-season (May–Jun) and after harvest (Aug–Sep).

    How far to trust it. The model learned from labelled fields inside this same area. In cross-validation it gets about 3 in 4 pixels right, so read small areas with care. Confidence is the model's own probability for the class shown. An independent accuracy check against ground-truth points is in progress.

    Seasons vary. The image periods follow the Central Plateau calendar: first season about March–June, a dry July, second season about August–November. Fields planted off this calendar, or left unplanted in a dry year, can be misread as Bare.

    Classes. “Bare” means not farmed and not tree-covered. It includes grassland, shrubland, rock, roads and buildings, because the model has no separate built-up class. “Potential farmland” is Farmland + Bare, the land that could be farmed.
